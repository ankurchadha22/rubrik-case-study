What should it cost?

The core package costs $12K per app license a year and earns about 70% gross margin

How I set $12K per license

  1. $15K+per human pen test

    Banks already pay for this job

    A human test of one complex app costs $15K to $35K, usually once a year.

  2. $12Kper license a year

    Rubrik gives four times the testing for less

    Four full Mythos tests a year plus weekly scans, for less than one human test. A certified tester still signs Rubrik's findings, so testing stays human-in-the-loop and is paid from the pen test budget.

  3. $14K+four AI pen tests a year

    It undercuts AI pen tests at the same pace

    Four AI pen tests at $3.5K to $6K each cost more than one license.

  4. 70%gross margin, my estimate

    It still makes money

    A license costs about $2,300 to serve, which clears the 60% margin the pilot gate requires.

I ruled out $20K, which prices like a human test, and $6K, which misses the 60% margin bar.

What could go wrong?

The biggest risk is a cheaper Claude Security, which could cap what banks will pay Rubrik

Likelihood and impact

High impactLow impact
Less likelyMore likely
1

Anthropic undercuts Rubrik with Claude Security

Response Claude Security scans code. Rubrik proves which attack paths are real across services and cloud, and can undo a bad fix.

2

Rubrik loses access to Mythos

Response The US government made Anthropic switch Mythos off for every customer for 19 days in June 2026. If that happens again, the product switches to Claude Opus 5.5, which catches slightly fewer flaws, 81% against 90% in Anthropic's test. Customer contracts promise tested apps, not a specific AI model, so a switch breaks no contract.

3

Scans cost more than I estimate

Response If scans use twice the tokens I expect, a license earns about 59%, just under the 60% gate. Pilots measure margin before Rubrik scales, and ranking files by risk first, as Anthropic's own scanner does, cuts token use.

4

Auditors still want a full human pen test

Response PCI and NYDFS want the yearly pen test done by a qualified person. A certified partner tester checks and signs Rubrik's findings, which keeps the test human-in-the-loop and lets the findings count toward it. If an auditor still wants a full separate test, Rubrik becomes extra spend, and the pilots show whether banks will pay for it.
